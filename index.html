Sample project · practice brief, synthetic data

Customer Order Data Cleanup

A simulated e-commerce brief: 30 messy kitchen-appliance orders with duplicate customers, mixed phone and date formats, email typos, missing categories and negative amounts. The goal was a clean CSV plus a short summary report.

ExcelTRIM / PROPERSUBSTITUTEVLOOKUPCOUNTIFPivot Table

What I did

  • Standardized customer names (casing, extra spaces) and phone numbers to 10 digits
  • Corrected email typos (.con, gmial) and converted mixed dd/mm and mm-dd dates to one format
  • Mapped category codes to product names with VLOOKUP
  • Found 8 duplicate orders by phone number using COUNTIF and kept the latest entry of each
  • Flagged negative amounts as possible refunds instead of deleting them

Result

30 → 22orders after removing duplicates
₹35,590total sales (net of refunds)
₹13,295top category: Mixer Grinder

Open items

  • 2 orders with no category code and 1 with no email are left marked "Missing" and listed in the report for the client to confirm

Deliverables

  • Clean_Orders.csv (cleaned dataset)
  • Summary report and pivot table (Excel)
Sample project · practice brief, synthetic data

Inventory Data Cleaning & Analysis

A simulated inventory brief: 146 messy records from 3 warehouses with duplicates, blank stock, wrong category spellings, negative prices and mixed date formats. The goal was clean data, reorder alerts and a short client report.

ExcelIFVLOOKUPPivot Table

What I did

  • Removed 6 duplicate records and standardized category names using the Product Master
  • Fixed negative prices, trimmed extra spaces and converted 3 date formats to one
  • Added Stock Value, Profit Per Unit and Reorder Status (OK / Reorder / Check Stock) with formulas
  • Used VLOOKUP to bring in supplier name and lead time
  • Built 2 pivot tables and a Top 5 summary (stock value and units sold)

Result

146 → 140records after removing duplicates
25items need reorder (Bengaluru 12, Chennai 4, Hyderabad 9)
39,312,591total stock value

Open items

  • 8 items with no Stock Qty are marked "Check Stock", 3 negative prices were changed to positive, and 4 records with no warehouse are marked "Unknown". The client needs to confirm these.

Deliverables

  • Cleaned inventory workbook (Excel)
  • Summary sheet, pivot tables and client report
Practice project · public Kaggle dataset, fictional client

Term Deposit Campaign Analysis (Bank Marketing)

A simulated brief from a bank’s marketing head: a phone campaign made many calls, but it was unclear who signed up for a term deposit. I cleaned 11,162 customer records, analysed them with SQL and built a 3-page Power BI dashboard to show who to call next time.

Google SheetsMySQLCTEWindow functionsPower BI

What I did

  • Checked duplicates, blank cells, “unknown” values and the pdays = -1 code, and recorded every decision in a Data Quality Log
  • Added age group and balance group columns in Google Sheets
  • Wrote 7 MySQL queries: conversion by job (RANK), age and education, number of calls (CTE), top months, previous outcome, and averages for yes vs no
  • Built a 3-page Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, charts, slicers and written insights

Result

91.3%conversion for customers who said yes in the previous campaign
53.4% → 22.4%conversion on the 1st call vs after 10+ calls
47.4%overall conversion across 11,162 customers

Open items

  • Some months look strong (Dec 91%, Mar 90%, Sep 84%) but have only 110 to 319 customers each, so the rates may not hold at higher volume. These are patterns in past data, not proof of cause.

Deliverables

  • SQL file with 7 commented queries and a Power BI dashboard (.pbix)
  • View the project on GitHub
